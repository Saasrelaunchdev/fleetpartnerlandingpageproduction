Questions.

Is this going to be another job for me?

No. We set it up with you, show your drivers the app, and check every week for the first 30 days that everyone is recording.

What do my drivers have to do?

Record their work as it happens: stops, CMR photos, refuelling. A driver's whole day is [n] · C23 steps. See it above.

Do I need GPS devices in the trucks?

Most carriers don't: position comes from the driver's phone. Need engine, fuel-level or temperature data? Keep your GPS hardware.

Can I keep my accountant or bureau?

Yes. FleetPartner doesn't replace them. They work from one record instead of a box of files.

Can I buy just part of it, like the map?

No. A system that sees part of your company can show you the wrong picture. Every plan is the whole system.

Does it work on my phone? On an iPhone?

Yes on Android, from Google Play: the same system as the web. iPhone: {TBD · A2}

Does it find loads or plan routes?

No. It doesn't connect to freight exchanges and doesn't plan routes.

Can it connect to my fuel card or accounting program?

Yes, built for your company as a quoted job, with your partner's consent and your own access.
